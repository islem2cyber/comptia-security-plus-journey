# Third-Party Risk Management

This lesson introduces the principles of Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) and explains how organizations identify, assess, monitor, and reduce risks introduced by vendors, suppliers, contractors, and other external partners.

As organizations increasingly rely on third parties for products, services, and cloud solutions, managing external risks has become a critical component of cybersecurity and enterprise risk management.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain the purpose of Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM).
- Identify common risks associated with vendors and supply chains.
- Understand the importance of due diligence before engaging third parties.
- Differentiate between common contractual agreements used with external organizations.
- Describe methods for continuously monitoring vendor security.
- Recognize best practices for reducing third-party and supply chain risks.

## Topics Covered

- Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)
- Vendor Risk Assessment
- Supply Chain Risk
- Due Diligence
- Vendor Selection
- Vendor Monitoring
- Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)
- Master Service Agreement (MSA)
- Business Partnership Agreements
- Continuous Risk Monitoring

# Security Compliance

This lesson introduces the role of security compliance within an organization's security program.

Security compliance focuses on ensuring that security practices, policies, controls, and processes align with applicable laws, regulations, industry standards, and organizational requirements.

Compliance is an important part of security governance because organizations must not only protect their systems and data, but also demonstrate that appropriate requirements are being followed.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain the purpose of security compliance.
- Understand the relationship between laws, regulations, standards, and organizational policies.
- Identify common compliance requirements relevant to information security.
- Understand the importance of protecting sensitive and regulated data.
- Recognize the role of documentation and evidence in demonstrating compliance.
- Understand how compliance requirements influence security controls and organizational practices.

## Compliance and Security

Compliance and cybersecurity are closely related, but they are not identical.

An organization can implement security controls without necessarily satisfying every applicable regulatory or contractual requirement.

Effective security programs therefore need to consider both:

- **Security requirements** — protecting systems, networks, users, and data.
- **Compliance requirements** — meeting applicable legal, regulatory, industry, and organizational obligations.

## Common Compliance Examples

Security programs may need to align with frameworks, standards, or regulations such as:

- GDPR
- HIPAA
- PCI DSS
- ISO/IEC 27001
- NIST frameworks
- Industry-specific requirements

The exact requirements depend on the organization's industry, location, services, and the type of information it handles.

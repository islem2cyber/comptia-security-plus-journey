# Audits and Assessments

This lesson introduces security audits and assessments and explains how organizations evaluate their security posture, controls, policies, and compliance requirements.

Audits and assessments help organizations identify weaknesses, verify that security controls are operating as expected, and determine whether organizational and regulatory requirements are being met.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain the purpose of security audits and assessments.
- Understand the difference between an audit and an assessment.
- Identify common types of security assessments.
- Understand the role of internal and external audits.
- Recognize the importance of evidence and documentation.
- Understand how assessment findings are used to improve security.
- Explain the importance of remediation and follow-up.

## Why Audits and Assessments Matter

Organizations need a reliable way to determine whether their security controls and processes are working effectively.

Audits and assessments can help organizations:

- Identify security weaknesses.
- Verify the effectiveness of security controls.
- Detect compliance gaps.
- Provide evidence to management and auditors.
- Support risk management.
- Improve security processes.
- Track remediation activities.

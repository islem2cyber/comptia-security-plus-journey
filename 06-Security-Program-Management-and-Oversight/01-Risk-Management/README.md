# Risk Management

This lesson introduces the core principles of the risk management process and explains how organizations identify, assess, document, and manage risks to protect their assets and business operations.

Risk management enables organizations to make informed security decisions by evaluating threats, vulnerabilities, potential impacts, and the effectiveness of security controls. It also introduces quantitative and qualitative methods for measuring risk and determining acceptable levels of exposure.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain the purpose of risk management.
- Differentiate between risks, threats, vulnerabilities, and assets.
- Describe the risk identification and assessment process.
- Understand quantitative and qualitative risk analysis.
- Calculate common risk metrics such as SLE, ARO, and ALE.
- Explain risk treatment strategies.
- Understand the purpose of a risk register.
- Differentiate between risk appetite, risk tolerance, and residual risk.

## Topics Covered

- Risk Identification
- Risk Assessment
- Qualitative Risk Analysis
- Quantitative Risk Analysis
- Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)
- Annual Rate of Occurrence (ARO)
- Annual Loss Expectancy (ALE)
- Risk Register
- Risk Treatment Strategies
- Risk Appetite
- Risk Tolerance
- Residual Risk

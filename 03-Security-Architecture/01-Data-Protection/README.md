# Lesson 01: Compare and Contrast Concepts and Strategies to Protect Data

## Overview

Data is one of an organization's most valuable assets and a primary target for cyberattacks. Protecting data requires more than simply preventing unauthorized access—it involves ensuring that data remains confidential, accurate, available, and recoverable throughout its entire lifecycle.

This lesson explores the fundamental concepts and strategies used to protect data in modern computing environments. It covers data states, classification, encryption, hashing, tokenization, masking, data loss prevention, key management, and other security controls that help organizations safeguard sensitive information and comply with regulatory requirements.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain the different states of data and their security requirements.
- Compare methods used to protect sensitive information.
- Understand data classification and labeling concepts.
- Differentiate between encryption, hashing, masking, and tokenization.
- Explain key management principles.
- Recognize Data Loss Prevention (DLP) technologies and strategies.
- Identify best practices for protecting data throughout its lifecycle.

## Why It Matters

Organizations generate, process, and store enormous amounts of sensitive information every day. A single data breach can lead to financial losses, legal penalties, operational disruption, and reputational damage. By understanding how data should be classified, protected, transmitted, stored, and securely destroyed, cybersecurity professionals can reduce risk, maintain regulatory compliance, and preserve the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of critical information.

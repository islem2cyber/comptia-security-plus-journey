# Resilience and Recovery

Modern organizations must be prepared to withstand failures, cyberattacks, and unexpected disasters while maintaining critical business operations. Security is no longer limited to preventing attacks—it also requires the ability to recover quickly and minimize operational disruption.

This lesson explores the principles and technologies that improve system resilience, ensure business continuity, and support disaster recovery. It covers high availability, redundancy, backups, fault tolerance, recovery planning, and resilience strategies commonly used in enterprise environments.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain the importance of resilience in cybersecurity.
- Differentiate between resilience, availability, redundancy, and fault tolerance.
- Understand high availability and load balancing concepts.
- Compare backup strategies and recovery methods.
- Explain disaster recovery and business continuity planning.
- Understand common recovery metrics such as RPO and RTO.
- Identify best practices for building resilient systems.

## Topics Covered

- Resilience Fundamentals
- High Availability (HA)
- Fault Tolerance
- Redundancy
- Load Balancing
- Backup Strategies
- Recovery Site Types
- Disaster Recovery (DR)
- Business Continuity (BC)
- Recovery Metrics (RPO & RTO)
- Resilience Best Practices

## Why This Matters

Organizations cannot prevent every outage or cyberattack. Well-designed resilience and recovery strategies reduce downtime, protect critical services, minimize data loss, and enable faster restoration of normal operations after an incident.

Understanding these concepts is essential for designing secure and reliable infrastructures and is a core objective of the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) certification.

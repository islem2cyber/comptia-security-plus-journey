# Social Engineering Quiz

## Overview

This quiz is designed to reinforce the key concepts covered in the **Social Engineering** lesson. It includes multiple-choice questions, true/false statements, and scenario-based questions that reflect the style of the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) certification exam.

---

# Multiple Choice Questions

## Question 1

Which of the following best describes **social engineering**?

A. Exploiting software vulnerabilities to gain unauthorized access

B. Manipulating people into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions

C. Cracking encrypted passwords using brute force

D. Scanning a network for open ports

> **Answer:** B

---

## Question 2

Which psychological principle is most commonly exploited when an attacker claims your account will be locked unless you act immediately?

A. Curiosity

B. Reciprocity

C. Urgency

D. Familiarity

> **Answer:** C

---

# Scenario-Based Questions

## Scenario 1

An employee receives an email appearing to come from the company's CEO requesting an urgent wire transfer to a new supplier.

**Question:** Which social engineering attack is being attempted?

> **Answer:** Whaling

---

## Scenario 2

A caller claims to be from the IT department and asks an employee to provide their password to resolve a technical issue.

**Question:** Which attack technique is being used?

> **Answer:** Quid Pro Quo (using impersonation)

---

## Scenario 3

A user receives a text message claiming that a package delivery failed and must click a link to reschedule delivery.

**Question:** Which attack is this?

> **Answer:** Smishing

---

## Scenario 4

An attacker compromises a popular industry website frequently visited by employees of a targeted company.

**Question:** Which social engineering attack is this?

> **Answer:** Watering Hole Attack

---

## Scenario 5

While entering a secure building, an employee unknowingly allows an attacker to enter by walking directly behind them before the door closes.

**Question:** Which physical social engineering attack is this?

> **Answer:** Tailgating

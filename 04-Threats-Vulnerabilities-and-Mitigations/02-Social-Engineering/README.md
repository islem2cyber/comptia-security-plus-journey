# Social Engineering

## Overview

Social engineering is the practice of manipulating people into performing actions or revealing sensitive information that compromises security. Rather than exploiting technical vulnerabilities, attackers exploit human psychology by leveraging trust, curiosity, fear, urgency, or authority.

Because many cyberattacks begin with human interaction, understanding social engineering is essential for identifying threats, reducing organizational risk, and strengthening an organization's overall security posture.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define social engineering and explain why it is effective.
- Identify common social engineering attack techniques.
- Differentiate between phishing, spear phishing, whaling, vishing, smishing, pretexting, baiting, and other attack methods.
- Recognize warning signs of social engineering attempts.
- Understand how attackers manipulate human behavior.
- Apply best practices to prevent and mitigate social engineering attacks.

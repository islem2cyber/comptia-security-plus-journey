# Threat Actors and Motivations

This lesson introduces the different types of threat actors encountered in cybersecurity and the motivations that drive their activities.

Understanding who attackers are and why they launch attacks helps security professionals assess risk, prioritize defenses, and respond effectively to security incidents.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain what a threat actor is.
- Identify common types of threat actors.
- Understand the motivations behind cyberattacks.
- Distinguish between internal and external threat actors.
- Recognize how different motivations influence attack techniques.

## Topics Covered

- What Is a Threat Actor?
- Common Threat Actors
- Threat Actor Motivations
- Insider vs. Outsider Threats
- Threat Actor Profiles

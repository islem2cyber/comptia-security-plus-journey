# Lesson 07: Explain Various Types of Vulnerabilities

## Overview

A vulnerability is a weakness in hardware, software, firmware, configuration, or organizational processes that can be exploited by a threat actor to compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability (CIA) of systems and data.

Understanding different types of vulnerabilities is essential for identifying security risks, implementing effective mitigations, and reducing the overall attack surface of an organization.

This lesson introduces the most common vulnerability categories encountered in modern IT environments and explains how they impact enterprise security.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

- Define what a vulnerability is.
- Differentiate between vulnerabilities and threats.
- Identify common software vulnerabilities.
- Recognize hardware and firmware vulnerabilities.
- Understand configuration and implementation weaknesses.
- Explain cloud and virtualization vulnerabilities.
- Identify supply chain and third-party risks.
- Understand the importance of vulnerability management.

## Why It Matters

Every successful cyberattack begins with the exploitation of one or more vulnerabilities. Organizations that continuously identify, assess, prioritize, and remediate vulnerabilities significantly reduce the likelihood of security incidents and strengthen their overall cybersecurity posture.

# Compare and Contrast Common Threat Actors and Motivations

Cybersecurity professionals must understand not only how attacks occur but also who is responsible for carrying them out and why they do so.

Threat actors vary widely in their capabilities, resources, objectives, and methods. Some seek financial gain, while others are motivated by political influence, espionage, ideology, personal revenge, or simply curiosity.

Identifying the type of threat actor and understanding their motivations enables organizations to assess risks, prioritize security controls, and respond more effectively to security incidents.

This lesson introduces the common threat actors encountered in modern cybersecurity, explores their motivations, and explains how their goals influence the techniques and attacks they employ.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Identify common threat actors.
- Compare the capabilities and objectives of different attackers.
- Explain the motivations behind cyber attacks.
- Understand how threat actors select their targets.
- Recognize the relationship between threat actors and attack techniques.
- Apply threat actor knowledge to real-world security scenarios.
